Sleep Mode

                        
For when the patient sleeps. Pauses only the eyes-closed alarm; all other alarms stay on. Once the patient has slept, waking (eyes open) or any selection sounds a chime here and is logged in Safety Alerts. Ends automatically after 12 hours. Nothing is sent off this device.
